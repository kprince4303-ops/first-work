About Us

Mr. Dipak, the owner of Deep Manglam Carpet – Parda Hi Parda, is a dedicated entrepreneur with 15 years of experience in the home décor and interior furnishing industry. He started this business in 2010 with a vision to provide high-quality carpets, curtains, bedsheets, sofa covers, mats, and doormats to customers at affordable prices. Through his hard work, commitment, and customer-focused service, he has built a strong reputation for trust and quality in the market. Today, Deep Manglam Carpet stands as a symbol of excellence, style, and reliability in home furnishing solutions. He is committed to offering the latest designs with superior quality to meet every customer’s needs. His goal is to make every home more beautiful, comfortable, and stylish.
