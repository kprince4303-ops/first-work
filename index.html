About Us

Mr. Dipak, the owner of Deep Manglam Carpet – Parda Hi Parda, is a dedicated entrepreneur with more than 15 years of experience in the home décor and interior furnishing industry. He started this business in 2010 with a clear vision to provide high-quality carpets, curtains, bedsheets, sofa covers, mats, and doormats at affordable prices. With continuous hard work, dedication, and a strong focus on customer satisfaction, he has successfully built a trusted name in the market. His commitment to quality products and honest service has helped him earn long-term relationships with customers. Deep Manglam Carpet – Parda Hi Parda is known for offering a wide range of modern and stylish designs that suit every home and lifestyle. The business focuses on providing durable, comfortable, and elegant home furnishing solutions that enhance the beauty of every space. Today, the goal remains the same — to deliver the best quality products, latest designs, and excellent customer service so that every customer can make their home more beautiful, comfortable, and stylish.
